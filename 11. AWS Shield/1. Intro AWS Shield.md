Introduction to AWS Shield

In this course, we’ll look at the AWS Shield. Today, every organization is moving their solutions (Web Applications, APIs) to the cloud environment, and so are the threats against your AWS applications. In this ever-changing environment, understanding and implementing robust defense mechanisms is crucial. Throughout the course, we will delve into the topics briefly outlined below.

Overview of AWS Shield
In our introductory segment, we'll unravel the layers of AWS Shield, exploring its fundamental purpose, core functionalities, and pivotal role in fortifying your digital assets against various cyber threats.

Setting Up AWS Shield
Whether you're safeguarding EC2 instances, Elastic Load Balancers, or leveraging the cloud's content delivery capabilities through Amazon CloudFront, we'll guide you through the essential configurations and best practices to fortify your defenses.

Monitoring and Incident Response
We will dive into the AWS Shield Dashboard – your watchtower against DDoS.

Advanced Strategies and Optimization
As we culminate our series, we venture into the realm of advanced AWS Shield features. Discover optimization strategies tailored to the unique characteristics of your applications and gain insights from real-world case studies. Elevate your defense game and fortify your AWS stronghold with lessons from the front lines of cybersecurity.

1. Latar Belakang
    Saat ini, setiap organisasi memindahkan solusi mereka (Web Applications, APIs) ke lingkungan cloud.
    Ancaman terhadap aplikasi AWS juga ikut meningkat.
    Dalam lingkungan yang terus berubah ini, pemahaman dan implementasi mekanisme pertahanan yang kuat sangat penting.

2. Gambaran Umum AWS Shield (Overview)
    Pada segmen pengantar ini, akan diuraikan lapisan-lapisan AWS Shield.
    Menjelajahi tujuan fundamental, fungsi inti, dan peran penting AWS Shield dalam memperkuat aset digital terhadap berbagai ancaman siber.

3. Setup AWS Shield
    Baik Anda melindungi EC2 instances, Elastic Load Balancers, atau memanfaatkan kemampuan pengiriman konten cloud melalui Amazon CloudFront.
    Akan dipandu melalui konfigurasi esensial dan praktik terbaik untuk memperkuat pertahanan.

4. Monitoring dan Incident Response
    Akan membahas AWS Shield Dashboard – menara pengawas (watchtower) Anda terhadap DDoS.

5. Strategi Lanjutan dan Optimalisasi (Advanced Strategies and Optimization)
    Di bagian puncak seri ini, akan menjelajahi fitur-fitur lanjutan AWS Shield.
    Menemukan strategi optimalisasi yang disesuaikan dengan karakteristik unik aplikasi Anda.
    Memperoleh wawasan dari studi kasus dunia nyata (real-world case studies).
    Meningkatkan permainan pertahanan dan memperkuat benteng AWS dengan pelajaran dari garis depan keamanan siber.
