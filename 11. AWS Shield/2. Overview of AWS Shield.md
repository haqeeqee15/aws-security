Overview of AWS Shield
What is DDoS?
A DDoS (Distributed Denial-of-Service attack) is a malicious attempt to disrupt the normal traffic of a website, online service, or network. It's like a digital mob that overwhelms the target with excessive traffic, rendering it unable to function properly. Here's how it works:

Imagine a busy restaurant :
Normally, customers come in, order food, and enjoy their meal. In a DDoS attack, it's like thousands of fake customers suddenly flooding a restaurant. Without placing any orders, they occupy tables, congest the kitchen, and prevent real customers from being served. Eventually, the restaurant becomes overwhelmed and stops serving everyone.

In the digital world :
The "restaurant" is a website, server, or network.
The "fake customers" are a flood of internet traffic from many different sources.
This traffic can be anything from simple ping requests to complex data packets.
When the target is overwhelmed, it slows down or crashes, preventing legitimate users from accessing it.

How DDOS Works?
A DDoS attack unfolds in several stages :
Preparation
1. Building the botnet : The attacker gathers an army of compromised devices, often through malware infections, exploiting vulnerabilities in IoT devices, or even paying individuals to lend their systems for the attack.

2. Command and Control : The attacker sets up a command and control server to issue orders to the botnet. This server tells the compromised devices when and where to launch the attack.

The Attack
1. Triggering the flood : The command and control server instructs the botnet to bombard the target with traffic. This traffic can take various forms, depending on the attack type:
    Volume-based attacks : A massive burst of simple requests overwhelms the target's bandwidth.
    Protocol attacks : Malformed or invalid requests exploit vulnerabilities in network protocols to crash the target server.
    Application-layer attacks : Complex requests target specific vulnerabilities in web applications to render them inaccessible.
2. Overwhelming the target : As the traffic surges, the target's resources become overwhelmed causing legitimate users to experience slowdowns, disconnections, or complete service outages.

Outcome
1. Disruption and damage : The attack's impact depends on its duration and intensity. It can range from mild inconvenience to significant financial losses and reputational damage.
2. Mitigation and response : The target works to filter out attack traffic, mitigate the damage, and identify the attacker.

Additional factors :
Attack vectors : DDoS attacks can aim at various aspects of the target's infrastructure, including its network, servers, or applications
Reflection attacks : In some cases, attackers use intermediary services to reflect traffic at the target, making it harder to distinguish malicious traffic from legitimate sources.
Evolving tactics : Attackers are constantly developing new techniques, so defense systems need to adapt and keep up.

What is AWS Shield?
AWS Shield is a service designed for managed protection against distributed denial of service (DDoS) attacks, ensuring the security of applications hosted on AWS. This service offers active detection and automatic mitigations, effectively reducing application downtime and latency. The advantage lies in its autonomy, eliminating the need for AWS Support to access DDoS protection. AWS Shield is available in two tiers: “Standard” and “Advanced”.

AWS Shield Standard
AWS Shield Standard offers automatic protection to all AWS customers at no extra cost. This standard service is designed to defend against common distributed denial of service (DDoS) attacks targeting websites or applications, particularly at the network and transport layers. When AWS Shield Standard is integrated with Amazon CloudFront and Amazon Route 53, it extends comprehensive availability protection, guarding against all known infrastructure attacks, including those at Layer 3 and 4.

For the underlying AWS services, AWS Shield Standard employs continuous network flow monitoring. This involves scrutinizing incoming traffic and utilizing a combination of traffic signatures, anomaly algorithms, and other analysis techniques to detect malicious traffic in real-time. While Shield Standard sets static thresholds for each AWS resource type, it does not offer personalized protection for your applications.

Furthermore, AWS Shield Standard incorporates inline attack mitigation with automated techniques. These techniques are seamlessly integrated into the service, protecting against common and frequently occurring infrastructure attacks. The mitigations are applied in real-time to safeguard AWS services, ensuring there is no noticeable impact on latency. Techniques such as deterministic packet filtering and priority-based traffic shaping are employed to automatically mitigate basic network layer attacks.

Shield Advanced
Building upon the network and transport layer protections offered by AWS Shield Standard, Shield Advanced provides additional layers of detection and mitigation against large and sophisticated Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks. It also delivers near real-time visibility into attacks and seamlessly integrates with AWS Web Application Firewall (WAF).

Detection Based on Traffic Patterns
AWS Shield Advanced offers personalized detection based on traffic patterns specific to your protected Elastic IP address, ELB, CloudFront, Global Accelerator, and Route 53 resources.

Health-Based Detection
Shield Advanced enhances responsiveness and accuracy in attack detection and mitigation. By defining a health check in Route 53 and associating it with a Shield-protected resource, the service detects attacks impacting application health more rapidly and at lower traffic thresholds. This improves DDoS resilience by preventing false positive notifications.

Advanced Attack Mitigation
Shield Advanced deploys sophisticated automatic mitigations for attacks targeting applications running on protected EC2, ELB, CloudFront, Global Accelerator, and Route 53 resources. By employing advanced routing techniques, it automatically enhances mitigation capacity to safeguard against DDoS attacks.

Automatic Application Layer DDoS Mitigation
Shield Advanced provides automatic protection against application layer (L7) DDoS events, mitigating them without manual intervention. It can create WAF rules to automatically counter an attack or activate them in count-only mode for a quick response without causing application downtime.

Proactive Event Response
With proactive engagement, the SRT contacts you directly when a Route 53 health check associated with your protected resource becomes unhealthy during a DDoS event. This facilitates quicker engagement with experts when your application's availability is affected by a suspected attack.

Protection Groups
Shield Advanced enables bundling resources into protection groups, offering a self-service method to customize the scope of detection and mitigation for your application. This improves detection accuracy, reduces false positives, and accelerates mitigation against multiple resources.

Visibility and Attack Notification
Shield Advanced provides complete visibility into DDoS attacks, with near real-time notifications through Amazon CloudWatch and detailed diagnostics on the AWS WAF and AWS Shield console or APIs. A summary of prior attacks is also accessible from the console.

DDoS Cost Protection
Shield Advanced includes DDoS cost protection, safeguarding against scaling charges resulting from DDoS-related usage spikes on protected EC2, ELB, CloudFront, Global Accelerator, and Route 53 resources.

Specialized Support
Customers on Business or Enterprise support plans enjoy 24/7 access to the SRT, providing expertise in triaging incidents, identifying root causes, and applying mitigations during DDoS attacks.

Global Availability
Shield Advanced is globally accessible across all CloudFront, Global Accelerator, and Route 53 edge locations, allowing for the protection of web applications hosted anywhere in the world.

Centralized Protection Management
AWS Shield Advanced customers can use AWS Firewall Manager to apply Shield Advanced and AWS WAF protections across their entire organization, ensuring uniform application of protections and automating security management.

In summary; AWS Shield Standard extends protection to all AWS customers, guarding against common and frequently encountered infrastructure attacks at layers 3 and 4. This includes defenses against SYN/UDP floods, reflection attacks, and others, ensuring the uninterrupted high availability of your applications on the AWS platform. AWS Shield Advanced delivers enhanced protections tailored for applications hosted on secured Amazon EC2, Elastic Load Balancing (ELB), Amazon CloudFront, AWS Global Accelerator, and Route 53 resources, specifically against larger and more sophisticated attacks. With continuous, flow-based monitoring of network traffic and active application monitoring, AWS Shield Advanced provides near real-time notifications for suspected Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) incidents. Moreover, it employs advanced attack mitigation and routing techniques to automatically counteract attacks. Customers with Business or Enterprise support can further benefit from 24x7 engagement with the Shield Response Team (SRT) for the management and mitigation of application layer DDoS attacks.

The DDoS cost protection for scaling shields your AWS bill against potential spikes in fees attributed to usage increases from protected Amazon EC2, Elastic Load Balancing (ELB), Amazon CloudFront, AWS Global Accelerator, and Amazon Route 53 during a DDoS attack. 

1. Apa Itu DDoS?
    DDoS (Distributed Denial-of-Service attack) adalah upaya berbahaya untuk mengganggu lalu lintas normal situs web, layanan online, atau jaringan.
    Ibarat kerumunan digital yang membanjiri target dengan lalu lintas berlebihan sehingga target tidak dapat berfungsi dengan baik.

Analogi restoran yang sibuk:
    Normalnya: pelanggan datang, memesan makanan, dan menikmati hidangan.
    Saat DDoS: ribuan pelanggan palsu tiba-tiba membanjiri restoran, mengambil tempat duduk, memenuhi dapur, mencegah pelanggan nyata dilayani.
    Akhirnya restoran kewalahan dan berhenti melayani semua orang.

Dalam dunia digital:
    "Restoran" adalah website, server, atau jaringan.
    "Pelanggan palsu" adalah banjir lalu lintas internet dari banyak sumber berbeda.
    Ketika target kewalahan, ia melambat atau crash, mencegah pengguna sah mengaksesnya.

2. Cara Kerja DDoS
Tahap Preparation:
    Building the botnet: Attacker mengumpulkan pasukan perangkat yang terkompromi (melalui infeksi malware, mengeksploitasi kerentanan di perangkat IoT, atau membayar individu untuk meminjamkan sistem mereka.
    Command and Control: Attacker menyiapkan server command and control (C2) untuk mengirim perintah ke botnet. Server ini memberi tahu perangkat yang terkompromi kapan dan di mana meluncurkan serangan.

Tahap The Attack:
    Triggering the flood: Server C2 menginstruksikan botnet untuk membombardir target dengan lalu lintas. Jenis lalu lintas bervariasi tergantung tipe serangan:
        Volume-based attacks: Ledakan besar request sederhana membanjiri bandwidth target.
        Protocol attacks: Request yang cacat atau tidak valid mengeksploitasi kerentanan dalam protokol jaringan untuk membuat server target crash.
        Application-layer attacks: Request kompleks menargetkan kerentanan spesifik di aplikasi web membuatnya tidak dapat diakses.
    Overwhelming the target: Saat lalu lintas melonjak, sumber daya target kewalahan menyebabkan pengguna sah mengalami perlambatan, pemutusan koneksi, atau pemadaman layanan total.

Tahap Outcome:
    Disruption and damage: Dampak serangan tergantung durasi dan intensitasnya (dari ketidaknyamanan ringan hingga kerugian finansial signifikan dan kerusakan reputasi).
    Mitigation and response: Target berusaha memfilter lalu lintas serangan, memitigasi kerusakan, dan mengidentifikasi penyerang.

Faktor tambahan:
    Attack vectors: DDoS dapat menargetkan berbagai aspek infrastruktur target (jaringan, server, atau aplikasi).
    Reflection attacks: Attacker menggunakan layanan perantara untuk memantulkan lalu lintas ke target, membuat lebih sulit membedakan lalu lintas berbahaya dari sumber sah.
    Evolving tactics: Attacker terus mengembangkan teknik baru, sistem pertahanan harus beradaptasi.

3. Apa Itu AWS Shield?
    AWS Shield adalah layanan yang dirancang untuk perlindungan terkelola terhadap serangan DDoS, memastikan keamanan aplikasi yang dihosting di AWS.
    Menawarkan deteksi aktif dan mitigasi otomatis, secara efektif mengurangi downtime dan latensi aplikasi.
    Keunggulan: otonomi, tidak memerlukan dukungan AWS untuk mengakses perlindungan DDoS.
    AWS Shield tersedia dalam dua tingkatan: Standard dan Advanced.

4. AWS Shield Standard
    Menawarkan perlindungan otomatis untuk semua pelanggan AWS tanpa biaya tambahan.
    Dirancang untuk mempertahankan terhadap serangan DDoS umum yang menargetkan website atau aplikasi, terutama di network dan transport layers (Layer 3 dan 4).
    Ketika diintegrasikan dengan Amazon CloudFront dan Amazon Route 53, memberikan perlindungan ketersediaan komprehensif terhadap semua serangan infrastruktur yang diketahui.

Cara kerja:
    Menggunakan continuous network flow monitoring (memeriksa lalu lintas masuk, menggabungkan traffic signatures, anomaly algorithms, dan teknik analisis lain untuk mendeteksi lalu lintas berbahaya secara real-time).
    Menetapkan static thresholds untuk setiap tipe resource AWS (tidak menawarkan perlindungan personalisasi untuk aplikasi Anda).
    Menggabungkan inline attack mitigation dengan teknik otomatis (deterministic packet filtering dan priority-based traffic shaping) untuk melindungi terhadap serangan infrastruktur umum dan sering terjadi.

5. AWS Shield Advanced
Dibangun di atas perlindungan network dan transport layer dari Shield Standard. Shield Advanced memberikan lapisan deteksi dan mitigasi tambahan terhadap serangan DDoS besar dan canggih, serta visibilitas near real-time ke dalam serangan dan integrasi mulus dengan AWS WAF.
a. Detection Based on Traffic Patterns
    Menawarkan deteksi personalisasi berdasarkan pola lalu lintas spesifik untuk resource yang dilindungi (Elastic IP address, ELB, CloudFront, Global Accelerator, Route 53).

b. Health-Based Detection
    Meningkatkan responsivitas dan akurasi deteksi dan mitigasi serangan.
    Dengan mendefinisikan health check di Route 53 dan menghubungkannya ke resource yang dilindungi Shield, layanan mendeteksi serangan yang mempengaruhi kesehatan aplikasi lebih cepat dan pada ambang lalu lintas yang lebih rendah.

c. Advanced Attack Mitigation
    Menerapkan mitigasi otomatis canggih untuk serangan yang menargetkan aplikasi pada protected EC2, ELB, CloudFront, Global Accelerator, dan Route 53.
    Menggunakan teknik routing canggih untuk meningkatkan kapasitas mitigasi secara otomatis.

d. Automatic Application Layer DDoS Mitigation
    Memberikan perlindungan otomatis terhadap DDoS events di application layer (L7) tanpa intervensi manual.
    Dapat membuat aturan WAF untuk secara otomatis melawan serangan atau mengaktifkannya dalam mode count-only untuk respons cepat tanpa menyebabkan downtime aplikasi.

e. Proactive Event Response
    Dengan proactive engagement, Shield Response Team (SRT) menghubungi Anda langsung ketika health check Route 53 yang terkait dengan resource yang dilindungi menjadi tidak sehat selama DDoS event.
    Memfasilitasi engagement yang lebih cepat dengan para ahli ketika ketersediaan aplikasi terpengaruh oleh serangan yang dicurigai.

f. Protection Groups
    Memungkinkan pengelompokan resource ke dalam protection groups.
    Menawarkan metode self-service untuk menyesuaikan lingkup deteksi dan mitigasi untuk aplikasi Anda.
    Meningkatkan akurasi deteksi, mengurangi false positives, dan mempercepat mitigasi terhadap banyak resource.

g. Visibility and Attack Notification
    Memberikan visibilitas penuh ke dalam serangan DDoS dengan notifikasi near real-time melalui Amazon CloudWatch dan diagnostic detail di konsol atau API AWS WAF dan AWS Shield.
    Ringkasan serangan sebelumnya juga dapat diakses dari konsol.

h. DDoS Cost Protection
    Melindungi terhadap scaling charges akibat lonjakan penggunaan terkait DDoS pada resource yang dilindungi (EC2, ELB, CloudFront, Global Accelerator, Route 53).

i. Specialized Support
    Pelanggan dengan Business atau Enterprise support plans menikmati akses 24/7 ke SRT, memberikan keahlian dalam triage insiden, mengidentifikasi akar penyebab, dan menerapkan mitigasi selama serangan DDoS.

j. Global Availability
    Shield Advanced dapat diakses secara global di semua edge locations CloudFront, Global Accelerator, dan Route 53, memungkinkan perlindungan aplikasi web yang dihosting di mana saja di dunia.

k. Centralized Protection Management
    Pelanggan Shield Advanced dapat menggunakan AWS Firewall Manager untuk menerapkan perlindungan Shield Advanced dan AWS WAF di seluruh organisasi, memastikan penerapan perlindungan yang seragam dan mengotomatiskan manajemen keamanan.

6. Ringkasan (Summary)
AWS Shield Standard:
    Melindungi semua pelanggan AWS.
    Melindungi dari serangan infrastruktur yang umum dan sering terjadi di layer 3 dan 4 (SYN/UDP floods, reflection attacks, dll).
    Memastikan ketersediaan tinggi aplikasi di platform AWS tanpa gangguan.

AWS Shield Advanced:
    Memberikan perlindungan yang ditingkatkan untuk aplikasi yang dihosting pada resource yang dilindungi (EC2, ELB, CloudFront, Global Accelerator, Route 53).
    Melindungi dari serangan yang lebih besar dan lebih canggih
    Dengan continuous flow-based monitoring dan active application monitoring, memberikan notifikasi near real-time untuk dugaan insiden DDoS.
    Menggunakan teknik mitigasi serangan canggih dan routing untuk secara otomatis melawan serangan.
    Pelanggan dengan Business atau Enterprise support dapat memanfaatkan engagement 24x7 dengan Shield Response Team (SRT) untuk manajemen dan mitigasi serangan DDoS di application layer.

DDoS cost protection:
    Melindungi tagihan AWS dari potensi lonjakan biaya akibat peningkatan penggunaan dari resource yang dilindungi selama serangan DDoS.
