Unauthorized Activities

AWS GuardDuty is designed to detect various types of suspicious and unauthorized activities within your AWS environment, including unsuccessful access attempts. When it comes to credential-related threats, GuardDuty monitors activities such as failed login attempts, which can indicate unauthorized or malicious access attempts.

GuardDuty analyzes different data sources, including VPC Flow Logs, AWS CloudTrail events, and DNS logs to identify patterns and anomalies in network traffic and account activities. If GuardDuty detects multiple failed login attempts or any other suspicious behavior that could indicate unauthorized access, it generates alerts to notify you. These alerts provide information about the attempted access, including the IP address, timestamp, and the targeted resource, allowing you to investigate further and take appropriate actions to secure your AWS environment.

GuardDuty's ability to detect unsuccessful access attempts is valuable for identifying potential attackers trying to gain unauthorized access to your resources. It allows you to respond proactively and strengthen your security measures to prevent successful breaches.

Port Scan
AWS GuardDuty can detect port scans using machine learning algorithms and threat intelligence feeds to analyze network traffic and DNS log patterns within your AWS environment. One of the activities it can detect is port scanning, which involves unauthorized attempts to discover open ports on your instances.

When GuardDuty detects a port scan, it generates an alert indicating the suspicious activity. These alerts include details such as the source IP address, the targeted instances, the ports scanned, and the timestamp. By monitoring network traffic patterns, GuardDuty can identify when an unusual number of connection attempts are made to various ports on your instances, signaling a potential port scanning activity.

Detecting port scans is essential for identifying potential attackers trying to find vulnerable services within your AWS infrastructure. GuardDuty's ability to recognize and alert you about these activities helps you take proactive security measures to block malicious IP addresses, strengthen security group rules, and protect your AWS resources from potential threats.
